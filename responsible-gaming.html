Responsible Gaming | hv7 Safe Play

Last updated: November 23, 2026

18+ This website is intended for adults aged 18 and older.

Our Commitment

At HV7 GAME, we believe gaming should be a fun, social experience. We are committed to promoting responsible gaming practices and providing tools to help players maintain control.

Age Restriction

HV7 GAME is intended for players aged 18 and above. Parents and guardians are encouraged to use parental controls to restrict access for minors.

Set Your Limits

We offer several tools to help you control your gaming experience:

  • Daily Play Time Limit: Set a maximum number of hours per day.
  • Notification Controls: Customize when and how often you receive alerts.
  • Self-Exclusion: Temporarily disable your account for a specified period.
  • Parental PIN: Secure settings with a PIN code.

Know When to Stop

Here are signs that gaming may be becoming problematic:

  • Gaming takes priority over work, school, or family responsibilities
  • You lose track of time spent playing
  • You spend more than you intended or can afford
  • Gaming causes arguments or relationship problems
  • You play to escape problems or emotions

Get Help

If you or someone you know has a gaming problem, help is available:

  • India: Call 1800-11-4000 (National Helpline for Mental Health)
  • Global: Visit Gambling Therapy

Parental Guidance

We encourage parents to:

  • Set up parental controls on devices
  • Monitor play time and content
  • Discuss responsible gaming with children
  • Use the parental PIN feature to lock settings

Stay Informed

Read our Privacy Policy to understand how we handle your data, and visit our Features section to learn more about responsible gaming tools.

For questions or support, email us at responsible@hv7.com.
